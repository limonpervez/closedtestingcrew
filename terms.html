1. What Closed Testing Crew does

Closed Testing Crew provides app testing services for Android developers. Our team of real human testers installs your app through your own Google Play Console closed-testing track and uses it on their personal Android phones for the duration of your package's testing cycle, posting daily proof (screenshots, session notes, bug reports) in a private WhatsApp group.
