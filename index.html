Frequently asked questions

What exactly is Google's 12-tester / 14-day rule?

New personal developer accounts must run a closed test with at least 12 testers opted in continuously for 14 days before Google will grant Production access. Google checks that testers actually engaged with the app during that window, not just that they opted in. We keep 12–20 real testers active every day of the cycle, so you pass that check on the first attempt.

Are the devices really daily-driver phones, not emulators?

Yes. Every tester is a staff member who installs your app on their personal, primary phone: devices with months of real usage history, SIM cards, and normal daily activity. We never use emulators, device farms, or freshly-reset "test-only" phones, which Google's systems can detect and flag.

What happens if my app is rejected anyway?

If Google doesn't grant Production access after our full 14-day cycle, we refund 100% of what you paid, with no forms or negotiations. In practice this is rare: 99.4% of apps we test are approved on the first attempt, and for the rest we usually re-run the cycle free after you fix the flagged issue.

Is my app and its data kept private during testing?

Your app is only distributed through your own Play Console closed-testing track, so Google controls access end to end. Our testers sign NDAs, never share builds outside the track, and we delete all artifacts (screenshots, logs, reports) from our systems 30 days after your launch, or immediately on request.

Can I shorten the 14-day period?

No, and be wary of anyone who says otherwise. The 14-day continuous window is Google's own requirement and cannot be legitimately skipped. What we do control is everything around it: same-day tester onboarding, zero gaps in daily activity, and a report ready the moment day 14 ends, so you lose no time beyond the mandatory window.

What if testers find bugs during the cycle?

That's a feature, not a problem. Each bug is logged with the device, OS version, and reproduction steps. You can push a fixed build to the same track mid-cycle; our testers update and keep testing without breaking the 14-day continuity. Two crashes caught in testing are two one-star reviews you never got.

How do I track testing progress?

Through your private WhatsApp group. The moment your order is confirmed, we create a group with our admin and all of your testers in it. Every day of the 14-day cycle they post timestamped screenshots, session notes and bug reports there, and you can ask any tester for feedback directly, one-on-one or in the group.
